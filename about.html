WHO WE ARE

Welcome to LB Computer Institute

Welcome to LB Computer Institute, a computer education and digital skills learning institute dedicated to helping students and learners build practical technology skills.

In today's digital world, computer knowledge is an important part of education, communication, productivity and professional development. Our objective is to provide learners with a structured environment where they can learn computer concepts and technology skills step by step.

Our available learning areas include computer fundamentals, Internet skills, MS Office, CorelDRAW, Photoshop, Hardware & Networking, PHP, DBMS, Python, ChatGPT, Artificial Intelligence and AI Prompt Engineering.

We encourage students to learn through practice and understand how their knowledge can be applied beyond the classroom.
